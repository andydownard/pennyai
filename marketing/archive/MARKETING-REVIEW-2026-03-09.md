### 3. Household Sharing Copy Reframe — APPROVED (CEO Override Needed on Tone)

**Sarah Chen (VP Product Marketing):**

> "This is the most important copy change of Tier 1. **Current copy is generic and feature-first:** 'Share a list with anyone — roommates, family, a partner. Everyone adds items, everyone sees updates live.'
>
> **New copy is pain-first and specific:**
> 'No more 'Did you get X?' Claim items as you add them. Your roommate knows milk is covered. Your partner doesn't buy duplicates. One shared list, clear ownership, less chaos.'
>
> **This works because:**
> - Opens with the problem (duplicate buys, unclear ownership)
> - Shows the solution in action (claiming prevents conflicts)
> - Ends with the emotional benefit (less chaos)
> - Uses concrete example (milk, bread) instead of abstract capabilities
>
> **This copy is a 'Gate 2' unlock** — visitors will see themselves in this scenario.
>
> **Copy passes the 'text to a friend' test.** ✓"

**Marcus Lee (Brand PM):**

> "Tone shift is intentional and correct. Old copy: generic, feature-focused. New copy: problem-focused, family-oriented, warm. This is Penny's voice.
>
> Specific example (Sarah claimed milk) makes it relatable and real. No jargon, no corporate speak."

**Andy (CEO):**

> "I approve the new messaging and positioning. This is the core story — household coordination = relief from miscommunication. Love the 'No more Did you get X?' hook. It's the same hero message we've been building.
>
> **Override note:** Tone is warmer and more conversational than the current website. This is intentional and good. Let's lean into it across all copy updates going forward.
>
> **Approved.** ✓"

**Status:** ✅ APPROVED — Ready for implementation (1 day)
